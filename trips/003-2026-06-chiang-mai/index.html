Journal 003 · June 18–23, 2026

Chiang Mai

Six days planned around Nimman cafés, local craft markets, the Old City and Lanna spa traditions.

Day by day

This journal follows the original itinerary. Individual stops are planned unless marked confirmed.

Day 1 Day 2 Day 3 Day 4 Day 5 Day 6
June 18

Nimman on arrival

Start in Nimman with MAYA and One Nimman. The plan brings together specialty coffee at ROAST8RY LAB, a meal at GINGER FARM Kitchen, mango desserts, and a stop at Let’s Relax.

June 19

Jungle zipline and Lanna spa

The planned route pairs KingKong ZipLine with The ii Spa and dinner at Ancient Beef Thai Cuisine. The visit to The ii Spa is confirmed.

June 20

Craft markets and studios

Browse local crafts at Jing Jai Market, continue to the artist studios at Baan Kang Wat, and wind down at Fah Lanna Spa in the Old City.

June 21

Sunday markets and the Old City

Start at Coconut Market, then head to Tha Phae Gate and the Sunday Night Market.

June 22

Food, shopping and a spa evening

The plan includes Nara Thai, shopping at Big C, mango drinks at Once Kitchen, and Divana Lana Spa.

June 23

Departure

Leave time for breakfast and an optional airport massage before departure.
